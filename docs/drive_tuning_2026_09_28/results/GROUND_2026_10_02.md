## Electrical health during the 26 runs
- No controller fault flags in any run. Bus voltage never below **10.1 V**. Peak motor current **27 A** straight, **33 A** in spins (current limit 50 A).
- Earlier in the session, **instantaneous** voltage steps (2 V, then +1.5 V twice) sagged the bus to **8.3 V** at 39 A per motor (no faults). They were dropped; all later tests use ramps.

## Result: straight lines (5 repeats each, mean error vs command)
Limits from the plan: ±2 % at 0.3 m/s and above; ±10 % below, and never stuck. 95 % band is 2·s/√5.

| Command | Forward L / R | Reverse L / R | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 m/s (150 RPM) | **−32.6 / −30.5 %** | −11.2 / −8.4 % | forward **FAIL**; reverse L marginal FAIL |
| 0.10 m/s (301 RPM) | **−12.9 / −11.5 %** | −3.3 / −1.9 % | forward **FAIL** (limit 10 %), reverse pass |
| 0.30 m/s | −1.0 / −0.5 % | +1.8 / **+2.4 %** | forward pass; reverse R marginal FAIL |
| 0.50 m/s | +0.3 / +0.9 % | **+2.3 / +3.4 %** | forward pass; reverse **FAIL** |
| 0.70 m/s | +1.9 / **+2.2 %** | **+3.5 / +3.9 %** | forward marginal; reverse **FAIL** |

- Bands are small (±0.1–0.5 % at speed, ±2–4 % at crawl): the errors are **systematic, not noise**.
- Ripple (speed noise) 10–15 RPM, i.e. about the encoder resolution; nothing is oscillating. Never stuck in straight lines (0 % of windows).
- Left and right match within **0.3–1.1 %** at 0.3 m/s and above; up to 3 % at crawl (right track a little faster).

## Result: slow spins in place (3 repeats each, both directions)
Command: ω = 0.3 rad/s (tracks ±332 RPM = 0.11 m/s) and ω = 0.1 rad/s (±111 RPM = 0.04 m/s), nominal track width 0.7366 m (multiplier 1.0).

| Spin rate | Delivered vs command | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 0.3 rad/s | **−31 to −43 %** on both tracks, both directions | **FAIL** |
| 0.1 rad/s | **tracks do not move** (96–100 % of windows stuck) | **FAIL** |

Peak track speed in the 0.3 rad/s runs was only 268–290 RPM against 332 RPM commanded (and the average over the steady window is lower, as above).

## What this means
1. **The pattern in straight lines matches the slow-ramp fit** (kV about 0.0021 V/RPM, not 0.0023; kS about 0.36 V forward and 0.26 V reverse, not 0.18 V): too much drive at speed (reverse +2–4 %), too little push at crawl (forward −31 %).
2. **Spinning in place on concrete is the real problem.** The tracks scrub sideways, the load is much higher, and the controller cannot deliver the commanded track speed: at 0.1 rad/s the robot does not turn at all, at 0.3 rad/s it turns about 60–70 % as fast as commanded. The speed loop is feedforward plus a small P with no integral term, so a heavy load leaves a large steady error.
3. **So the PID/feedforward should be re-tuned before the turn multiplier is calibrated.** A multiplier measured now would absorb this delivery shortfall (as the old 1.19 did).
