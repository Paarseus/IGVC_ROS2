# ZED X ball-joint mount (final)

Mounts a ZED X to the front T-slot of a 2020 aluminium extrusion via a clamped 22 mm ball joint.

| File | Part | Print orientation |
|---|---|---|
| `RailBallBase.stl` | Plate + 22 mm ball, bolts to the rail | Rail face down, ball up |
| `SocketCap.stl` | C-ring cap that clamps the rear of the ball | Split face down |
| `BackBracket.stl` | Bolts to the camera back, holds the socket cup | Camera face down |

STLs are already in print orientation; no supports needed.
Sliced for Bambu P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, PLA, 0.16 mm layers (~1 h 50 min, 44 g for all three).

## Hardware
- 2x M5x10 button head (ISO 7380) + 2x M5 T-nuts: base to rail
- 4x M4x10 SHCS: bracket to camera back
- 3x M4x25 SHCS + 3x M4 nuts: clamp (nuts press into the cap's rear pockets)

## Notes
- Cap/cup clamp gap is 2.0 mm. A ~1-1.5 mm gap should remain when the ball is locked;
  if the faces touch, the ball is undersized.
- Cup cavity is 22.1 mm (tight). Sand lightly if the ball does not seat.
