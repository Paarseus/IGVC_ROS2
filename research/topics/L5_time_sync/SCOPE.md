# L5 — Time synchronization: scope map

**Mapped:** 2026-09-28 (step 1, map). `sources/` was empty at mapping time, so no subtopic is covered yet.

## Overview
Time synchronization is the problem of knowing *when* each measurement was actually taken, on one common time base, so that a fusion filter or controller combines data that describe the same instant. The field draws on four bodies of work. (1) **Clock theory and network synchronization** from distributed computing and time-and-frequency metrology: clock models (offset, skew/frequency error, drift), stability measures such as Allan deviation (NIST TN 1337), remote clock reading (Cristian 1989) and the protocols that discipline computer clocks — NTP (Mills 1991, RFC 5905) and its implementations ntpd and chrony, IEEE 1588 PTP with hardware timestamping (linuxptp: ptp4l, phc2sys, ts2phc), and GNSS-derived time via a pulse-per-second (PPS) signal (RFC 2783 PPS API; u-blox timing notes). (2) **Effects of timing error on estimation**: the first-order error "position error = velocity × time error", its full treatment for GNSS-aided INS (Skog & Händel 2011) and the Syncline model; delayed and out-of-sequence measurements in Kalman filters (Larsen et al. 1998; Bar-Shalom 2002, 2004). (3) **Temporal calibration in robotics**: estimating fixed or slowly varying offsets between sensors — passive host-side filtering of arrival times (Olson 2010; TICSync, Harrison & Newman 2011), batch continuous-time estimation (Furgale, Rehder & Siegwart 2013, Kalibr), filter- and optimisation-based online estimation (Kelly & Sukhatme; Li & Mourikis 2014; Qin & Shen 2018), and hardware triggering (Nikolic et al. 2014; VersaVIS 2020). (4) **Practice in ROS 2 and on devices**: header stamps and clock types (system, steady, ROS time, `use_sim_time`), tf2 buffers and extrapolation limits, `message_filters` synchronisation policies, latency tracing (ros2_tracing), driver stamping choices, and the time features of specific sensors and computers (Xsens SampleTimeFine/UTC and SyncIn/SyncOut, Velodyne PPS + GPRMC top-of-hour stamps, ZED image timestamps, USB-serial latency, Jetson Orin PTP hardware). Product-specific material is one subtopic, placed last. The command-pipeline latency budget belongs to C3 and fusion configuration to L4; this topic covers the time base and stamping those rely on.
