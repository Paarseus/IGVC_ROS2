All Terrain Rotatrucks PRO

      When material handling is critical to perform in your day to day job, the Rotatruck PRO range is ideal hand truck for your needs. For parcel & wholesale delivery, bottle shops, bulk retail, white goods or furniture, the Rotatruck PRO is built with quality Magline aluminium components and offers options such as different handles, noses and accessorises. With a 250mm (10") puncture proof rear wheel the All Terrain Rotatruck navigates with ease over rough surfaces and through difficult terrains.
