The Smarter Hand Truck for Effortless Handling

When it comes to moving loads safely and efficiently, not all hand trucks are created equal. The Rotatruck hand truck range is designed with patented multi-directional omni wheels, allowing you to do your work with ease. Unlike traditional hand trucks, Rotatruck reduces strain, increases productivity, and gives you unmatched control when handling heavy or awkward loads.

•    59% less effort to pull back a load
•    100% less effort to carry or balance it — the load is fully supported
•    78% less effort to step up a kerb — no reversing or awkward lifting

The Rotatruck Hand Truck is Easier, Quicker, and Safer and pays for itself in months, not years.

Why Choose a Rotatruck Hand Truck?

Rotatrucks are engineered to do more than just move boxes. Our unique design transforms the way materials are handled:

Omni-directional movement – glide forward, backward, and sideways with precision.

Reduced lifting effort – pivot loads without strain and minimise manual handling injuries.

Improved productivity – save time and energy when transporting goods.

Durability you can rely on – built tough for industrial, commercial, and everyday use.

 

Designed for Safety and Comfort

A Rotatruck hand truck isn’t just about moving loads—it’s about moving them smarter. The ergonomic design helps reduce the risk of back and shoulder injuries by shifting much of the load’s weight onto the wheels, not the operator. This makes every move safer, smoother, and less physically demanding.

 

The Right Hand Truck for Every Job

Whether you’re in warehousing, retail, delivery, or simply need a reliable tool for everyday handling, there’s a Rotatruck to suit your needs. Our range includes modular designs and conversion kits, so you can adapt your hand truck to match specific tasks without replacing the entire unit.
