## Description

### **Note: As of 03/03/25, all Thrifty Swerve kits ship with an[updated top plate](https://www.thethriftybot.com/products/thrifty-swerve-v2-top-plate-kraken-x44-compatible) compatible with the Kraken x44 motor.**

**Shipping Now! Check out upgrades and compatible Thrifty Swerve products[here](https://www.thethriftybot.com/collections/thrifty-swerve).**

Thrifty Swerve is an omni-directional swerve module designed for FRC. It works with motors either inverted or non-inverted and has a number of advantages for teams. Battle tested by teams [1706](https://www.thebluealliance.com/team/1706) and [1684](https://www.thebluealliance.com/team/1684) during the entire 2024 season, this module strives to provide teams an affordable, flexible and robust option that will serve you well on the playing field.

Features include:

  * All steel gear speed reduction for azimuth and drive - no belts

  * 6 gear ratio options included - swappable without module disassembly

  * Thrifty Absolute Magnetic Encoder included

  * Extra 10-32 threaded mounting holes on the main and top plates

  * Base plate made from 7075 Aluminum

  * Inverted motors located fully above the bottom frame rail protecting them
  * No 3D printed parts

  * Symmetrical design allows for azimuth placement on either side




The module ships unassembled, and the following components are not included:

  * Drive motor (WestCoast Products Kraken X60, REV Robotics NEO or REV Robotics NEO Vortex recommended)

  * Steering motor (WestCoast Products Kraken X60, REV Robotics NEO or REV Robotics NEO Vortex recommended)

  * [1.5” Wide Tread](https://www.thethriftybot.com/products/1-5-wide-black-3-ply-neoprene-rubber-tread-10-foot-qty?_pos=1&_sid=31a26fe40&_ss=r)
