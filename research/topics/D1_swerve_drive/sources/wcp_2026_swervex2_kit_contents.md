# Kit Contents

Purchasing this product has been simplified to buying a "[Complete Kit](/welcome/gearboxes/wcp-swerve-x2/general-info/kit-contents/complete-kits.md)" or "[Individual Components](/welcome/gearboxes/wcp-swerve-x2/general-info/kit-contents/individual-components.md)".

The "[Complete Kits](/welcome/gearboxes/wcp-swerve-x2/general-info/kit-contents/complete-kits.md)" will have all the necessary parts for assembly minus motors.

"[Individual Components](/welcome/gearboxes/wcp-swerve-x2/general-info/kit-contents/individual-components.md)" is an option for teams if:

* They already have some of the parts on hand, such as gears, bearings, or raw material
* Want to purchase spare parts
* Upgrading from one version to another version
