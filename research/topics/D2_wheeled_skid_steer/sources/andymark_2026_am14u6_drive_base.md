**This is the "AM" - "ONE" - "FOR" - "YOU"!**

The perfect base for prototyping robotic applications as well as being part of
the final design! This large format, infinitely configurable drive platform
was designed to be built with performance in mind. The AM14U6 drive chassis
has three main configurations (Long, Square & Wide) as starting options. The
drop center wheel allows for smoother turning ability, while maintaining
stability. This is the 6th generation of this popular chassis now includes "on
grid" #10 clearance holes that make it much easier to interface popular upper
structure options such as [pre-drilled tubing](/products/pre-drilled-box-tube-
extrusion) and [gussets](/products/1-2-in-pitch-gussets).

This product ships as an un-assembled kit. It features [HiGrip
Wheels](/products/higrip-wheels-options), [Toughbox Mini S
Gearboxes](/products/toughbox-mini-s), and HTD [Belts](/categories/belts).

Assembly and cutting of aluminum is required. **[See the Assembly
Videos](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLJsMMdkkIYsJ6R765xcZ1C3D8CmJd3GB4&si=XeDzGAveBVvUPKJE).**

  
  

If this chassis is being used for the _FIRST_ Robotics Competition, please
consider the robot perimeter rules and size this chassis accordingly. The
manual helps clearly show where to make cuts for different sizes, you can
adapt your cuts to fit as needed.

### Gear Ratio:

In the 2025 _FIRST_ ® Robotics Competition the KOP Chassis has 8.46:1 Gear
Ratio. On this page you can select all possible gear ratios. If you would like
to know more about gear ratios and how they affect your robot, watch our [How
Do I Change Motor Speed and Torque?](https://youtu.be/HpW5GRSC-
KY?si=o8l822UTFGBxZlS0) video.

### Various Configurations:

Built on the same great features of previous AM14U family chassis, this can be
configured into many different perimeter, [wheel](/products/am14u5-2023-wheel-
upgrades), and gearbox configurations. There are literally thousands of
possible playing configurations.

    * Smallest Frame size: 24.3 in. x 27 in.
    * Largest Frame size: 32.3 in. x 31 in.

It is shipped as a Kit with plates that can build in a large square
configuration. This makes it the largest possible size and allows for the user
to trim the length and width of the chassis to become a long, wide, or a
smaller square to fit different the size constraints of each specific year.
The assembly instructions show how it can be changed, be sure to check out the
assembly videos as well.

### Encoder Options:

The AM14U6 does not include encoders. The included Toughbox Mini S Gearboxes
ship with an [output shaft](/products/toughbox-s-series-1-2-in-hex-output-
shaft) that has holes in the ends for optional included [1/4 in. diameter
steel dowels (am-1323)](/am-1323) that are compatible with the [US Digital E4T
Encoder (am-3132)](/am-3132) and the [REV Through Bore Encoder
(am-4650)](/am-4650). Teams also have the option of purchasing a [magnetic
dowel (am-3505)](/am-3505) to replace the steel dowel allowing the use of the
[SRX Magnetic Encoder (am-3445)](/am-3445). In order to use this encoder on
the Toughbox Mini S, our [SRX Magnetic Encoder Mounting Hardware Kit
(am-3565)](/am-3565) must also be used to mount each encoder.

### Control Options:

To control this drive, you will need a power and control system like the
[Electronic Bundles kit, roboRIO, Spark Max, 4 CIM motors, and PDP
(BCRX4CP)](/products/electronic-bundles?sku=BCRX4CP). Many options are
available!

The chassis requires a minimum of two [CIM style motors (am-0255)](/am-0255),
but we recommend four CIMs to be competitive in situations like heavy robots,
high traction, and pushing match situations that put a lot of load on the
motors. You will also need at least one battery. We recommend the [2 Pack of
MK Batteries (am-0844)](/am-0844), and the [3 Bank SB50 NOCO Battery Charger
(am-4968_3)](/am-4968_3). There are many options available here as well.
