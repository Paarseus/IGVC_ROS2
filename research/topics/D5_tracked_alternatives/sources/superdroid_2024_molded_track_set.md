## Description

"This is a pair of All Terrain reinforced 4 inch wide tracks for UGVs. They have a mesh reinforcement
and are molded rubber with no splice. These treads have small timing cleats in the center of the
track. They are 13mm square spaced 48mm apart. Standard length is 45 pitches/cleats making the track
length 2160mm."

## Features and specifications

- "These tracks will require your own wheel design." The same tread is used on SuperDroid's own HD2
  robot, minus the internal guide cleats — a buyer must design their own guide cleats and wheels to
  use this track set.
- Multi-ply tread, aggressive black rubber all-terrain pattern, 4 inches wide.
- No splice — "They are molded to be continuous."
- Overall tread thickness about 0.61 inch with the traction knobs; 0.25 inch without the knobs.
- Weight of one tread: 4.1 lb.
- Track circumference approximately 2160 mm (standard, 45 cleats/pitches).
- Traction lugs: 8 mm thick, 10 mm tall, spaced every 48 mm.
- Custom length available: minimum 20, maximum 70 cleats/pitches, with 4-8 week lead time.
