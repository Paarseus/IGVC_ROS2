## Overview

The Raptor Track Drive (am-4020) is a tank track system featuring timing belt treads, designed as an
improved successor to AndyMark's earlier Rhino Track Drive. It is described as "an efficient, low
profile tank track system utilizing timing belt treads."

## Key specifications

**Structure:**
- One-piece 1/8-inch aluminum side plates (left and right versions)
- Low-profile design with flanges for bumper mounting
- Dual turnbuckle tensioning mechanism contained within the plates (tensioning the belts does not
  change the robot's frame perimeter)

**Drivetrain components:**
- Pulleys: five polycarbonate pulleys per module (20-tooth Track Drive Pulley design, model am-3298a)
- Sprockets: 22-tooth #35 aluminum sprockets
- Tensioning: balanced dual turnbuckle mechanism
- Tread options: blue nitrile roughtop tread (am-3288) or black urethane flat tread (am-4017), both
  timing-belt-type treads

**Gearbox compatibility:**
The system accommodates mounting of a Toughbox Mini, EVO Shifter, EVO Slim for CIMs, or EVO Slim for
775s gearbox to the inside plate.

## Configuration options

- Full chassis: two modules plus connecting extrusions ("Peanut" and "Walnut"); three foot lengths of
  each extrusion ship with two modules so the builder can cut them to length
- Individual left or right half-modules sold separately
- Two tread-material choices per module

## Noted limitation (customer review on the product page)

One customer review on the product page states that the "Peanut" extrusions "are not robust enough"
for high-impact FRC competition use, citing denting and eventual drivetrain failure after field
impacts.

## Included items

Each module ships unassembled with hardware, side plates, bearings, sprockets, pulleys, and one belt.
